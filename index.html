Shop Parameters

Drilled-hole, thread, external-thread, and hex-socket rates are in seconds/mm and multiplied by finished part length in millimeters; second-operation time remains per operation.

Material is prorated by stock length used (finished part length plus length allowance) × quantity. Custom tooling is charged at the customer-paid share; PITA applies to labor only, and Rush to material and labor. Scrap/reuse, finishing, tax, and other special tooling are not included.
